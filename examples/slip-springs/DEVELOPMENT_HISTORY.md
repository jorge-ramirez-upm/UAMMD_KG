# Development history

This is a decision-oriented history; the individual reports and commits remain the detailed record.

1. **Stage 0–2B:** established the isolated UAMMD/KG example, independent Gaussian Rouse reference, exact dimensions and diffusion checks, configurational stress, direct-lag analysis, and validated Correlator6 streaming. N100 terminal evidence and the Stage 2B.1 host report close the Gaussian reference work. Production-server performance was deliberately left hardware-specific.
2. **Stage 4A.1–4A.2:** introduced the separate nonlinear finite-rest-length semiflexible backbone. The exact radial/conditional-orientation initializer and open-chain force law were preserved as CPU authority. GPU force/update, diagnostics, precision paths, and bounded observations were implemented. The frozen Stage 4A.2 campaign reported 204 user-host experiments with zero automated failures, while explicitly limiting acceptance to tested cases; see [STAGE4A2_REPORT.md](STAGE4A2_REPORT.md).
3. **Stage 4A.3A–4A.3B.2B:** derived the positive affine stress, added CPU references and an opt-in six-channel GPU/streaming adapter, reused the existing correlator, and added bounded operational/readiness tooling and compact scalar output. Scientific host execution and terminal nonlinear stress claims remain separate from implementation acceptance.
4. **Stage 4A.4:** extended timestep comparisons from N=8 to N=32, κ=0 and κ=10. The baseline was `2.5e-5`; comparisons at `1e-3` (N8) and `5e-4` (N32) retained static/dynamic uncertainty, H(0) amplitude diagnostics, exact-lag limitations, and unresolved missing distributions. The preparation and recovery work is recorded by commits `2c55374` and `a8b44b8`; N32 GPU outputs were recovered read-only after correcting their midpoint-inclusive second-half histogram contract.
5. **Stage 4A.5:** added a CPU-only Hessian preselector. It is explicitly heuristic and does not alter dynamics or thresholds. The recorded N8 κ10 example gives Q99 187.950 and `dt_trial=2.6603e-4` at c=0.05, versus the harmonic estimate `1.7857e-4`.
6. **Close-backbone point:** the repository now has a tested nonlinear backbone, stress infrastructure, bounded timestep evidence, and a documented list of unresolved scientific decisions. The next phase is design of slip-spring physics, not a claim that those rules have already been selected.

The current consolidation commit should be read with the historical Stage reports. External host outputs are identified as such in Stage 4A.4 protocols and recovery receipts; they are not silently represented as committed raw trajectories.
