# Ee=6 joint E2 assessment at absolute 4M

## Scientific decision

**Recommend qualified acceptance of the three authenticated 4M endpoints for production initialization, subject to separate user approval for formal registration.** No additional E2 duration is justified by the present joint evidence. This is a documented scientific recommendation, not registered acceptance, selection, activation, publication, preparation or authorization to launch production.

The late network change is a bounded excursion with an observed reversal inside the existing histories. Peripheral satellites do not accumulate persistently. Chemical populations, intra/inter balance, degree moments, motifs and accepted exchange have no sustained common direction across the interval choices. Conformation has a small downward ensemble sequence and a larger r001 offset, which remain an explicit initialization-bias uncertainty. Qualified suitability accepts that limited uncertainty; it does **not** certify stationarity or complete decorrelation. Slow topology is not proven fully decorrelated.

## Interval analysis and initial transient

All initial association frames, including the unbound step-zero state, are retained in the exports and full-history figures. 0–1M is preparation context, not a stationary comparator. Consecutive 500k and 1M intervals use left-exclusive/right-inclusive frame and event selection. IATs, slopes and 5/10/20-block SEMs are recalculated for each interval; errors are never averaged. Additional shifted contrasts compare 1.5–2.5M with 2.5–3.5M and overlapping 2–3.5M with 2.5–4M. Final 250k halves probe reversal, with explicitly limited shape resolution. All windows, replica paths, estimates and exploratory comparisons are retained in [summary.json](summary.json), [window estimates](window_estimates.csv), [paired comparisons](paired_comparisons.csv), and [terminal halves](terminal_halves.json).

Paired Student-t intervals have df=2 and only three independent histories. They are exploratory, unadjusted, and overlapping comparisons are correlated. Including zero neither establishes stationarity nor bounds an initialization bias. IAT assumes local stationarity and uses the existing first-nonpositive-lag heuristic; effective sample estimates and block sensitivities cannot rule out slower unseen modes.

## Peripheral-network explanation

The final 500k giant decrease is reproduced exactly: −0.000748/−0.001110/−0.000556 in r001/r002/r003, or 0.748/1.110/0.556 stars outside the giant on average. Its paired mean is −0.00080467 ± 0.00069888, an exploratory interval excluding zero. Connected components increase by 0.484/1.112/0.352. This local flag warrants decomposition, not automatic extension.

For late frames the graph satisfies components = 1 giant + isolated stars + non-singleton satellite components, and stars outside the giant = isolated + satellite stars. No singleton is counted as a non-singleton satellite. These identities were checked frame by frame.
