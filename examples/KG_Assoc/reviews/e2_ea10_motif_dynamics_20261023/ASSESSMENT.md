# Ea10 motif dynamics through 88M

Review date: 2026-10-23. Starting clean local/remote `KremerGrest` SHA:
`54bf76d1596bdb40472c9455b6f47c2d90a75131`.

**Recommendation: retain Ea10 as scientifically unresolved, with no automatic
further simulation.** The evidence is compatible with slow structural excursions;
it does not establish stationary regime occupancies or a persistent preparation
transient. The investigation weakens an interpretation of r003's late increase as
a unique or irreversibly accumulating network structure. Its broader elevated
triangle/shape excursion nevertheless remains open at 88M. This is a scientific
hold, not a numerical failure or proof that its endpoint is unsuitable.

The established E2 standard concerns qualified `production_initial_state`, not
proof of thermodynamic equilibrium, complete topology renewal, or an ESS threshold.
No new acceptance threshold is introduced. The three original Ea4 parent families
remain the preparation units; time windows and recurrent motifs are correlated
observations, not additional independently prepared replicas.

receipts, original approval/source records, native builds and executables authenticate.
Source maps are checked against Git blob bytes at their historical commits, rather

numerical validation equals its previously independently replayed validation exactly.
The nine segments cover 8–24M, 24–56M and 56–88M in each family: **240M existing MD
steps**, **2,400,000 recorded oracle sweeps**, and nine actual initial readback results.
This investigation executes **zero MD/GPU steps** and does not repeat a GPU comparison.
It authenticates existing strict numerical validation and independently rereads every
accepted event for graph/motif analysis; it does not claim a new full endpoint-numerics
replay. Prior numerical checks and their limitations remain authoritative.

The new triangle replay reproduces L2 at **240,009 diagnostic frames**, final triangle
identities from the previous independent ledger, graph/component counts, and exact
triangle-time and star-incidence conservation. Event order resolves individual motif
births, deaths and reappearances. Sampled conformation remains the previously recorded
system-mean Rg² and center–terminal squared distance. No unavailable particle trajectory,
per-star conformation history or geometric triangle orientation is reconstructed.

Residence, recurrence gaps, lifetime censoring, ACF/IAT, block SEM and lagged statistics
are confined to a single fresh-GJ segment. Boundary-present triangles have unknown
age and are excluded from incident-lifetime risk sets; boundary survivors are
right-censored. Identity-ever-seen sets alone cross segment boundaries, to distinguish
new connectivity from returns. Static full-history distributions are descriptive

The replay has 1k-step structural samples and exact reaction-sweep event timing.

burn-in. High-L2 cutpoints 35, 40 and 45 are exploratory descriptions, not inferred

The high values are new relative to

r003's latest 8M means remain **30.90,32.71,36.81,40.26**. Its final two 8M
windows spend 22.83% and 61.71% at L2≥40. The final 4M mean is 41.65.
That coarse enrichment is real even though there are many local reversals.
There are **nine** L2≥40 visits in 56–88M: eight complete and one right-censored.
The longest complete visit is **83.1647–85.7335M (2.5688M)**; another lasts
1.6114M. The final visit begins at 86.2614M and is censored after **1.7386M**.
By comparison, r002's longest completed ≥40 visit in 8–24M lasts **1.9088M**.
r003's three ≥45 visits all finish, lasting only 0.0761–0.1587M. Its extreme
upper tail is neither absorbing nor frozen.

The broader band tells a different part of the story. r003 completes a ≥35 visit
at **74.1568–79.6638M (5.5070M)**, spends only 0.0765M below it, and then stays

completed ≥35 visits of **7.80M (r001)** and **9.46M (r002)** in 24–56M.
r003's broad visit is therefore not unprecedented in duration, but its own sustained
return from the latest broad regime has not been seen. Recrossing 40 cannot be
substituted for such a return. Neither one fitted slope nor a threshold crossing
settles long-time stationarity.

## Identity renewal, residence and spatial interpretation limits

| Latest 32M | Triangle gains / losses | Recurrent triangle births since 8M | Closing edge previously seen since 8M | Positive incident median lifetime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| r001 | 94 / 84 | 7 | 10 | 9.44M |
| r002 | 89 / 78 | 2 | 4 | 8.43M |
| r003 | 108 / 99 | 7 | 12 | 7.83M |

A triangle birth is the closure of an existing distinct-neighbor wedge by an edge.
Parallel sticker associations and intra-star bonds do not create distinct-star
triangles. For r003, **96 of 108** triangle births use a closing star-pair edge
not seen previously since 8M; **101 of 108** are newly encountered triangle
identities. These counts weight triangle births: one edge may close more than one
triangle. Most growth is associated with new neighbor connectivity rather than
repeated closure of a few old partners. Event records resolve graph closure, not
whether bead-scale motion caused a particular closure.

r003 visits 135 triangle identities in the latest segment. Only three of the 34
initial triangles survive continuously; four are in the final set of 43. The net
gain is nine against 207 births/deaths. r001/r002 also gain ten/eleven net triangles
in this segment. A rising population is not persistence of the same motifs.
RECURRENCES.csv preserves observed identity-specific gaps, with zero gaps explicitly separable;
ABSENCE_EPISODES.csv also records terminal non-return intervals as right-censored,
so the observed-return table is not mistaken for a complete waiting-time distribution;
TRIANGLE_EPISODES.csv preserves all observed and censored episodes.
Across all three segments there are only 11 observed motif-return gaps per family,
against 205/217/235 terminal censored non-return intervals. The complete-return
subset cannot establish a recurrence-time distribution or rapid motif revisitation.

Positive incident Kaplan–Meier medians in the latest segment are 7.8–9.4M; r003's
75th percentile is **16.34M**. The r001/r002 latest 90th percentiles are not
identifiable. Initial unknown-age motifs are excluded, deaths precede censor removals
at tied durations, and censored episodes are not treated as completed lifetimes.
Recurrent, interacting motifs are correlated; these are descriptive survival curves,
without independent-event confidence intervals or an exponential lifetime model.
An unconditional incident median does not predict the remaining life of an already
aged population regime.

High-L2 participation is distributed. During r003's ≥40 periods, **226 distinct
stars** participate; the top five and top twenty contribute **5.56% and 18.33%**
of exact triangle-incidence exposure. Corresponding shares for r001's latest segment
are 6.20%/18.77%, and r002's initial high band 6.78%/20.08%. At high-L2 diagnostic
frames, no family has more than two triangles incident on one star. r003 averages
121 participating stars in its high band: hundreds participate over time, not all
1000 simultaneously. This rejects dominance by a few persistent star labels, but
cannot establish spatial homogeneity without geometric data.

The high band combines more closed three-star paths and fewer parallel association
pairs with modestly more compact average stars. Mean coordination hardly changes.
This is consistent with redistribution of network connectivity, rather than simply
more neighbors per star. Increased clustering/participation are partly algebraic
consequences of L2 and cannot serve as independent corroboration. Full component-size
histograms are retained; the largest component remains near 998–1000 stars. There
is no new macroscopic fragmentation. Peripheral occupancy is correlated with the
regime; sparse satellites do not define a separate structural phase.

At matched integer L2 on common high-state support, r003 minus r001 has Rg²
**−0.00331**, L1 **−1.42**, and intra **−3.31**; against r002 the differences
are **+0.02724**, **+0.97**, and **−5.74**. Matching uses symmetric equal-family
common-support weights, not a sampling-error estimate. Thus high-L2 network counts
are comparable, and r003's shape is especially close to r001's high states, but the
joint states are not identical. Intra populations and clustering retain family
structure. Scalar/network agreement cannot establish microscopic configuration
equivalence. See MATCHED_L2.csv and joint-distribution plots.

Occupancy mixing materially affects correlations. r003's L2–Rg² correlations in
its three segments are **+0.038,+0.259,−0.178**; pooling all 80M gives **+0.116**.
In the newest segment, removing high/low group means leaves correlation −0.100;
about 62% of the total covariance is between the groups. For center–terminal
squared distance the between-group fraction is ~73%; for L1 it is ~55%. L2–L1
correlation is −0.551 over the full newest segment but **+0.311 in the final 8M**.
These sign and occupancy changes preclude a universal causal reading.
Lag diagnostics at ±0.1/0.5/1/2M remain descriptive and segment-local; broad curves
and changed short-window signs do not identify a causal ordering.

## Timescales and what the data cannot distinguish

r003's latest L2 block SEM rises **0.80,1.12,1.55,2.10** for 1M/2M/4M/8M
blocks. There are only four 8M blocks. Its full-32M first-nonpositive ACF estimate
gives IAT **4.32M**, ESS **3.70**; the final-8M estimate gives **0.76M**, ESS
**5.26**. All families show substantial segment/subwindow sensitivity. These
stationarity-dependent estimates are uncertainty diagnostics, not validated errors
on equilibrium means or automatic acceptance tests. No conventional trend p-value,
independent-window test or minimum ESS is used.

Evidence **for slow excursions (A)** includes broad cross-family state overlap,
completed high visits, reversals in the full history, active motif renewal and
moderate recurrence. Evidence **compatible with a long transient (B)** includes
r003's changing occupancy weights, the unclosed broad elevated visit and coupled
conformation/L1 changes. Neither distinguishes a stationary process with long
residence times from a preparation-biased evolving distribution. With only three
inherited families, incomplete slow-mode sampling and segment restarts, **C
(insufficient information to distinguish A from B)** is the supported conclusion.
No irreversible drift or Ea-dependent equilibrium structural difference is established.

## Decision questions and value of another calculation

1. **Unresolved mode:** family-specific residence and return of the coupled
   triangle/parallel-bond/conformation regime, rather than survival of individual
   motifs, bulk bond count, or a small frozen star cluster.
2. **Distinct r003 regime?** It has a distinguishable late high-occupancy band
   relative to its own past. The data do not establish a new physical basin or phase.
3. **Similarity to r001/r002:** substantial L2 and participation overlap, particularly
   close r001 high-state mean shape; conditional intra/clustering/family differences
   prevent declaring identical configuration distributions.
4. **Transitions/reversals?** Yes: eight completed ≥40 visits and a completed 5.51M
   ≥35 visit in r003's latest segment, plus returns in earlier segments and other
   families. Its final 8.26M broad elevated visit is still censored.
5. **Value of r003 alone?** A targeted observation could resolve that return and
   materially improve its qualified-initialization assessment. Expected information
   gain is moderate and conditional, not guaranteed substantial: another high-band
   record could leave the same ambiguity. It would not prove ensemble stationarity.
6. **What interval has a rationale?** If Ea10 is essential to the scientific claims,
   an optional **order 8–16M additional observation of r003** is motivated by
   7–9.5M completed broad-band dwells, the current 8.26M censored visit, its ~7.83M
   incident motif median/~16.34M 75th percentile and ~4.3M full-segment L2 IAT.
   Eight million steps would provide two 4M comparisons over a comparable dwell;
   sixteen million would provide four. These are an information-scale estimate,
   not a forecast of escape, a required duration, an extension plan or permission.
   A useful outcome is sustained return and joint-observable redistribution, or
   evidence that continued occupancy changes persist; merely reaching a time limit
   or an ESS would not settle qualification. No rationale supports automatically
   extending all three families again.
7. **Accept only r001/r002?** Their previously targeted modes are better resolved,
   but the existing formal standard binds an exact three-history system. Splitting
   formal acceptance would change that standard and does not cure r003 uncertainty.
   Retain the triplet hold; neither history is newly accepted here.
8. **Manuscript decision:** if Ea10 is not essential, proceed with the **fourteen
   qualified systems** and identify Ea10 as an explicitly unresolved high-barrier
   case. Present these trajectories as finite-time structural dynamics, not
   equilibrium distributions. Prefer this transparent limit over automatically
   purchasing another long trajectory to obtain a desired acceptance outcome.

Historical Ea4 preparation/provenance limitations, unproven equilibrium invariance
of the finite-step coupled algorithm, residual parent memory and rare-component
sampling remain qualifications. The investigation does not claim equilibrium or
initiation-bias removal, and does not make complete decorrelation a prerequisite.
